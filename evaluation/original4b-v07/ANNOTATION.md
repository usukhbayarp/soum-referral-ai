# Clinician handoff and later training gate

Open `clinician-review.html`: exact original source, provisional expected facts, saved A/B proposals from both prompt versions, prior 1.7B comparisons, and specific adjudication questions. No model response is a label. The blank review boxes are a worksheet, not persistent storage or an approval action. Copy corrections into a separately retained reviewed artifact; do not mark the displayed fixture approved merely by opening it.

`annotation-drafts.json` is a compact envelope around **both existing** experimental representations, not a new model-output schema. It retains original note, case/family ID, development split, fictional origin, explicit unreviewed status, blank reviewer/date, form/output/prompt/segmentation/annotation versions, provisional A roles and B facts, evidence references and missing/absent/not-assessed questions. `expected-facts.json` and each run's source mapping provide exact original unit offsets; quote resolution continues to use the unchanged segmenter and strict repeated-quote handling. Do not edit historical expected facts to fit a model output.

Clinician work: correct medication role, name, dose, route, time and frequency links; retain patient-reported attribution; distinguish explicit absence, not asked/not assessed and undocumented; mark omissions and ambiguous statements; choose defensible evidence. Separate harmless capitalization/spacing mismatch from wrong clinical associations, while retaining the strict validator result. Record reviewer, date, explicit status, corrected targets and reasons. Do not infer administration from a patient taking medication or from a prescription. Review role/fact expectations too.

All four families have been used for development. They must remain development families; no relabeling as held-out tests. No test content was accessed. Separate clinician-reviewed training families are still required. Family-overlap checks and explicit review gates in the existing canonical exporter remain reusable. Its source-assignment targets are compatible with the unchanged production extraction contract, not automatically with A or B. Selecting either experimental representation requires a versioned adapter to its already-frozen schema, reviewed-target validation and a separate training export; this milestone intentionally exports **no training JSONL**.

Once reviewed train/development data arrives: validate only that authorized material and its family metadata; do not infer review from file presence, inspect held-out content, or launch training automatically. Keep the base model and prior tags for rollback. No tuned candidate exists yet.
