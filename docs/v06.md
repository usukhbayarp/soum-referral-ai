The six-section clinician-designed template is integrated as **experimental-0.6**, with active prompt **source-id-v06-1**, output contract **source-id-v06-1**, and unchanged segmentation **sentence-lines-1**. Default model remains **qwen3:1.7b**. This is an unofficial experimental referral summary, not an approved national form or replacement for 13А.

Detailed values remain manual; model suggestions must be checked against the note. No training, conversion, paid hosting, held-out evaluation or clinical-quality improvement is claimed.

## Source and reference integrity

The supplied template says v0.6. The supplied completed example says v0.5; its human-readable reference is retained unchanged at `evaluation/dev002-v06/reference-original-v05.txt`, independently of `source-note.txt`. Only the exact eleven S01–S11 source paragraphs, joined by newlines, reach the extractor. No completed answers, grading instructions or explanatory notes are included. No later clinician-added facts exist in this example; review/signature/stamp fields are initially blank.

`provenance.json` records document hashes, original and integration versions, `reviewed: false`, and source hash. The source is **1,620 characters / 33 deterministic units**, preserved without truncation. `source-map.json` explicitly maps S01–S11 to internal IDs; these numbering systems are not assumed identical. `cases.json` contains provisional source assignments using the clinician's supplied mapping, not paraphrase-string targets. It is AI-generated, fictional, development-only and awaiting final clinician review. All variants must retain underlying case family `DEV-002`, remain in development, and stay out of held-out evaluation. The exporter excludes unreviewed examples.

Arrival (S01 10:00), initial examination (S04 10:05), and current observation (S07 11:00) have separate fields. The completed reference's wording is not allowed to overwrite the source. Full dates are never silently synthesized: the clinician must verify the association between S01's date and a time-only source; ambiguous times stay time-only. The pain-change suggestion references S04 + S06 without claiming causal treatment response.

## Automatic versus manual behavior

All automatic text is assembled **verbatim from validated source IDs**. It is a proposed classification, not semantic verification. This list describes implementation capability, not successful DEV-002 accuracy.

- Automatically proposed narrative fields: complaint/course (`history`), medical history (`medical_history`), regular medication (`regular_medication`), documented assessment/diagnosis with uncertainty (`diagnosis`), comorbidity (`comorbid`), allergies (`allergies`), examination (`examination`), investigations (`investigations`), referral reason (`referral`), requested receiving-clinician action (`requested_action`), attached results (`attached`), pending-results statement (`pending_results`), and documented follow-up plan (`plan`).
- **Source suggestions only**, separate from detailed values: identity/encounter, initial observations, current observations, doctor-documented referral type, administered treatment/procedures, and pain change. A selected paragraph is explicitly not proof of each subfield. Structural reuse between initial/current or regular/administered groups raises a review flag; this is not a comprehensive contradiction detector.
- **Manual only:** patient/demo ID, real identifier, age/sex, note date/time, sending facility/clinician/phone, receiving facility/department; referral-type selection (no default); optional diagnosis codes; arrival time; each initial/current observation time, condition, BP, pulse, respiratory rate, SpO₂, temperature and oxygen device/flow; every treatment-row name, dose/unit, frequency, route, administration/last-dose time and documented response; conditional confirmations, pending-result owner/method, transport, prior agreement, review notes/doctor/time/signature/stamp. There is no automatic medication tuple assembly, ICD inference, date joining, urgency decision or signature.

Treatment rows are editable and independently identify medication/procedure and single/repeated administration. Single doses do not require frequency. No response is generated from pain-score change. Long textareas expand. Printing uses the six sections, explicit attribution, review warnings, unbroken ordinary-sized treatment rows, repeated fictional ID and page numbers in supported Chromium print engines. A row longer than a physical page may necessarily span pages; arbitrary printer/browser support is not claimed. The tested long-text fixture and three treatment rows fit correctly.

Extraction provenance and clinical meaning are separate: model-not-found, extraction failure and manual addition remain distinct; the clinician can explicitly record documented presence, documented absence, not assessed/not asked, not documented, and uncertainty/conflict. These meanings are not inferred from nonempty text. Manual edits preserve original extraction evidence but never claim it verifies the edit. New extraction proposals remain separate. Source changes retain manual work and require individual reconciliation; late responses, including delayed example loads, cannot populate a newly started case. New referral/example replacement clears the case. Editing invalidates approval and removes print content/header. Missing information can be acknowledged rather than fabricated.

## Completeness checks

Core warnings cover identification (demo ID is sufficient), sender/facility/phone, destination, reason, requested action, doctor-documented referral type, complaint/course, assessment, current condition/time, administered treatment or explicit no-treatment confirmation, and allergies. Real identifier, ICD codes and comorbid diagnoses are not mandatory failures.

Conditional rules use explicit clinician selections, not keyword inference. They check medication name/dose-unit/route/time, frequency for repeated doses only, oxygen device/flow if used, applicable investigation completeness, pending-result owner/method **only when pending is confirmed**, and transport/agreement only when applicable. A clinician-confirmed “no pending results documented” suppresses pending-owner/method warnings. Unknown triggers request review. Qualitative, not-performed and pending tests do not automatically require numeric units or completed results. These warnings do not decide whether transfer is allowed or certify comprehensive clinical completeness. Approval still requires review + explicit acknowledgment, and is blocked by active extraction, unresolved reconciliation or over-limit manual content.
